# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming place for researchers, writers,
developers, and contributors of all experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and research confidentiality.
- Use inclusive language.
- Accept correction and take responsibility for mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person’s private information or project content.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or bad-faith participation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other
contributions that violate this policy, and may temporarily or permanently ban
participants for serious or repeated violations.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner’s GitHub contact
channel. Do not include sensitive project content unless it is necessary and
safe to share.

---

## 中文说明

本项目欢迎不同经验背景的研究者、写作者和开发者。请保持尊重、耐心和建设性，
讨论观点和代码而非攻击个人，并尊重他人的隐私与研究保密要求。

骚扰、威胁、歧视、公开他人隐私、恶意破坏和垃圾信息均不可接受。维护者可以
删除违规内容，并对严重或重复违规的参与者采取临时或永久限制。
